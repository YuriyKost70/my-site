About the project

Residential apartment

This project is a modern apartment designed for family living with a focus on space planning and functional reorganisation.

The main task was to redesign the internal layout to improve functionality, circulation and everyday comfort.

The floor plan was reworked to create clear zoning between private and shared areas, optimise the use of space and adapt the apartment to family needs. Attention was given to spatial logic, ergonomics and practical usability.

The result is a well-structured family apartment with a clear and functional layout, ready for further implementation.
