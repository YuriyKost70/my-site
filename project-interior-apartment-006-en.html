About the project

American classic apartment

This project presents a family apartment designed in an American classic style.

The main objective was to create a comfortable and timeless interior that combines traditional aesthetics with modern functionality.

The planning and interior solutions were developed around the needs of family living, with clear zoning, balanced proportions and practical everyday scenarios. Particular attention was given to symmetry, material selection and the calm, elegant visual language associated with American classic interiors.

The result is a refined and functional apartment concept that provides lasting comfort and a cohesive stylistic identity.
