About the project

Two-storey family house

This project presents a two-storey residential house designed in a modern style with an open-plan ground floor.

The main focus was to create a spacious and flexible living area that supports everyday family life.

The ground-floor layout integrates the living, dining and kitchen areas, with the fireplace serving as a central architectural element. The design emphasises spatial continuity, clear circulation and a balanced relationship between the functional zones.

The result is a contemporary family house with a flexible and well-organised layout that combines architectural clarity with a comfortable living atmosphere.
