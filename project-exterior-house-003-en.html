About the project

Modern House

This project focuses on the design of a courtyard for a small modern residential house, including a detached grill area and the surrounding outdoor environment.

The goal was to create a compact yet functional outdoor space suitable for everyday use and social activities.

The layout was planned to clearly define functional zones, ensure comfortable circulation, and integrate the grill area as an independent but cohesive element of the overall courtyard design. Attention was given to proportions, usability, and visual balance.

The result is a well-organized outdoor space that complements the architecture of the house and supports comfortable outdoor living.
