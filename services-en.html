Services

What we do

The key sections of a design project that make renovation clear and manageable, from space planning and lighting to specifications and 3D visualisations.

01

Space planning package

Site measurements, demolition and construction plans, furniture layouts with actual dimensions and circulation routes.

Outcome: a clear spatial logic before work begins.
02

Lighting design

Lighting groups, dimming, morning/work/evening/night scenes and switch layouts.

Outcome: lighting designed around different daily scenarios.
03

Electrical and low-voltage systems

Power outlets, cable routes, installation heights, control points and precise electrical locations.

Outcome: electrical systems without improvised decisions on site.
04

Plumbing and HVAC

Fixture locations, service risers, ventilation, air conditioning and underfloor heating circuits.

Outcome: coordinated technical solutions for all systems.
05

Wall elevations and details

Room-by-room wall elevations, floor and ceiling layouts, doors, stairs and bespoke joinery.

Outcome: details that can be executed accurately.
06

Specifications

Materials, furniture and lighting, including brands, quantities and suitable alternatives.

Outcome: a clear basis for procurement.
07

3D visualisation

Key views of the future interior, allowing design decisions to be made with visual clarity.

Outcome: visual verification of decisions before implementation.
Deliverables

What you receive

This is the final set of materials you can use to communicate with contractors, prepare cost estimates and move confidently into implementation.

  • a structured design project album in PDF format
  • drawings arranged in a logical sequence for use on site
  • 3D views that visually confirm the approved design decisions
  • specifications with product names, quantities, brands and suitable alternatives
  • materials for preliminary budget calculations
  • clear instructions for builders, joiners and other contractors
